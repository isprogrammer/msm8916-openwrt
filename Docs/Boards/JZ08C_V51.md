# JZ08C V51 (experimental)

## Build identity and hardware evidence

- Build alias/profile: `jz08c-v51` / `generic-jz08c-v51`
- Compatible: `jz08c-v51,generic`
- DTS: `msm8916-generic-jz08c-v51` (patch 869), based on `msm8916-ufi.dtsi`
- MSM8916, 512 MiB RAM; stock eMMC is 7,634,944 sectors (3,909,091,328 bytes)
- Stock MPSS reservation: 78 MiB at `0x86800000`
- Battery Phase 1 adds an experimental simple-battery profile: 2100 mAh from the pack label, 4.20 V charge/design ceiling (intentionally below its 4.35 V label), and 3.40 V minimum from the stock charger cutoff. The stock DT has one 1500 mAh / 4.20 V battery-data profile; its five-temperature OCV curve is reused as a provisional voltage-to-capacity estimate, not as proof of the replacement cell's characterization.
- Charge current is capped at 1.0 A, matching the conservative value used by MF800B, MiFi V12, and JZ02. Charge termination is 100 mA and factory resistance is 210 mΩ, converted from stock DT values. No precharge value exists in the stock profile.
- The owner measured a fixed 45 kΩ third lead to negative; this is treated as a likely battery-ID resistor. The BMS node has no `batt-therm` IIO channel and no thermal charge-control logic is added. The BMS uses the 25 °C OCV table when no thermistor is supplied; it will not report battery temperature. Whether the PMIC's hardware temperature comparator has a usable thermistor connection remains unverified.

## Storage and recovery

The image uses the standard MSM8916 GPT layout, sized for this board's
7,634,944-sector eMMC. Boot, rootfs and rootfs_data use p13, p14 and p15.
SDC2 remains disabled. The standard migrate flow saves the seven radio and
calibration partitions (fsc, fsg, modemst1, modemst2, modem, persist and sec)
before rewriting the GPT, then restores them by partition name. Keep the
owner's complete raw stock dump off-device; restore that full dump through EDL
to return to stock. The stock aboot does not support `fastboot boot`; the
OpenWrt bootloader step installs the image's lk2nd-capable aboot so supported
fastboot recovery commands become available. Do not rely on RAM boot under the
stock aboot.

## Display and controls

The ST7735S is attached to BLSP QUP4 SPI (`0x78b8000`, TLMM 12–15), with
DC on TLMM GPIO116 and reset on GPIO118. JZ08C uses `panel-mipi-dbi-spi` and a
board-specific compatible to load the stock init sequence converted to MIPI DBI
firmware format. The 128×128 active area uses the stock address-window offsets
(2, 3); panel rails are L17 (2.85 V) and L6 (1.8 V). MPP4 supplies the stock
40 mA fixed-on backlight sink. The panel driver is packaged only for JZ08C;
SPI QUP and PMIC MPP pinctrl are already enabled in the msm89xx kernel.

After flashing, check `dmesg | grep -Ei 'drm|panel|spi'` and confirm `/dev/fb0`
exists. A 128×128 RGB565 test pattern is 32 KiB; write one with
`dd if=/dev/urandom of=/dev/fb0 bs=4096 count=8` for a visible but noisy test.

Power is the PM8916 KPDPWR key. Reset is PM8916 RESIN, mapped to KEY_RESTART;
the stock input layer reports it as KEY_F1. The stock gpio-leds nodes on GPIOs
1, 20, 22 and 23 are SIM-control lines, not indicator LEDs; they are retained
only under their stock SIM labels. No indicator LED mappings are included.

## Flashing

Use the matching `*-generic-jz08c-v51-*` artifacts and the included `flash.sh`.
Migration uses the standard seven-partition radio/calibration backup in `saved/`
before the first write. A separate full raw dump is strongly recommended and
is the recovery path to restore stock. Retain a copy off-device. The board has
a physical EDL recovery path. Follow the generic flashing guide and use the
GPT and firmware images from this board's release.
